Privacy Policy

Bird Identifier: AI Vision · Last Updated: September 2026
Overview: Bird Identifier ("we", "our", or "the App"), operated by ORX Labs, is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is processed, and your control over your data when using our iOS application.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. We only process data necessary to provide accurate bird identification and a seamless birdwatching experience:

  • Camera & Photo Library: With your permission, the App accesses your camera or photo library solely to allow you to photograph or select bird images for species identification.
  • AI Image Data: When you initiate an identification, the selected photo is transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS to our AI processing partner (Google Gemini API). Photos are analyzed ephemerally in real time and are not permanently stored or used to train public AI models.
  • Local Sighting Journal: Saved birds in your Life List and sighting records are stored locally on your device using on-device storage.
  • Subscription & Purchase Data: Subscriptions are managed securely via Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat. We never receive or store credit card or bank account details.

2. Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • To identify wild birds, songbirds, raptors, and waterfowl using visual AI recognition.
  • To provide detailed taxonomic data, IUCN conservation status, wingspan, diet, vocalization notes, and native breeding range maps.
  • To verify active subscription entitlements and unlock Pro features.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We only utilize trusted service providers adhering to industry-standard security:

  • Google Gemini API: For AI vision and taxonomic identification.
  • RevenueCat: For managing in-app subscription receipts and entitlements.
  • Apple Inc.: For payment processing, app distribution, and StoreKit transactions.

4. User Rights & Consent Control

You have full control over your data and device permissions:

  • Camera & Photos: You can revoke camera or photo library access at any time in your iOS Settings > Privacy > Photos / Camera.
  • AI Consent: You can toggle or revoke AI data processing consent at any time within the App under Profile > AI Privacy.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete any saved bird sightings directly from your Life List with one tap.

5. Children's Privacy

Bird Identifier is designed for general audiences and nature enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
