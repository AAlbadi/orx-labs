Privacy Policy

Bug & Spider ID: AI Vision · Last Updated: September 2026
Overview: Bug & Spider ID: AI Vision ("we", "our", or "the App"), operated by ORX Labs, is committed to safeguarding your privacy. This policy outlines how information is collected, processed, and protected when you use our iOS application.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. We only collect and process data essential to providing accurate entomological and arachnological visual identification:

  • Camera & Photo Library: With your explicit iOS permission, the App accesses your camera or photo library solely to enable photographing or selecting insect, spider, and bug images for classification.
  • AI Image Data (Ephemeral Processing): When an identification is submitted, the image is transmitted securely via TLS/HTTPS encryption to our AI vision processing infrastructure (Google Gemini API). Images are analyzed ephemerally in real time to generate taxonomy and safety data, and are not retained, permanently stored, or used to train public machine learning models.
  • Local Field Journal & Sightings: Your saved specimens, custom sighting notes, and life list collections are stored locally on your device via on-device storage.
  • Subscription & Purchase Data: In-app purchases and subscription entitlements are managed securely through Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat. We never receive, process, or store your credit card or financial information.

2. Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed strictly for the following functional purposes:

  • To visually classify insects, spiders, butterflies, moths, beetles, mantises, dragonflies, bees, wasps, and other arthropods.
  • To provide detailed taxonomic classification (Order, Family, Scientific Binomial), danger and venom levels, lifespan, ecological habitat, and native geographic distribution.
  • To verify active subscription status and unlock Pro capabilities (unlimited scans, offline field guides, high-resolution dossiers).

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, monetize, rent, or trade your personal data. We partner only with trusted enterprise service providers adhering to industry-standard security and strict data privacy agreements:

  • Google Gemini API: For real-time visual neural inference and taxonomic knowledge extraction.
  • RevenueCat: For receipt validation, subscription lifecycle management, and entitlement verification.
  • Apple Inc.: For secure in-app purchase billing, StoreKit transactions, and iOS app distribution.

4. User Rights & Consent Management

You maintain complete control over your data and device permissions at all times:

  • Camera & Photos: You can modify or revoke camera and photo library access anytime via iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos / Camera.
  • AI Data Processing Consent: You can review and toggle your AI data processing consent inside the App at any time under Profile > AI Privacy.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete any saved specimen or sighting record from your Field Journal with a single swipe or tap.

5. Children's Privacy

Bug & Spider ID is built for nature explorers, students, and enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under 13 years of age.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory standards. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.
