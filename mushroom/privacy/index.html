Privacy Policy

Mushroom ID: AI Fungi Scanner · Last Updated: September 2026
Overview: Mushroom ID ("we", "our", or "the App"), operated by ORX Labs, is committed to safeguarding your privacy. This policy details our data practices, ephemeral AI processing, and security protections for our iOS application.
Critical Safety & Informational Notice: Mushroom ID is designed exclusively for educational, ecological, and recreational study. Automated image recognition cannot replace an in-person examination by a certified toxicologist or mycologist. Never ingest or handle wild fungi based on digital scans.

1. Information We Collect

We practice privacy-by-design and only process data strictly necessary to deliver accurate fungi identification and safety insights:

  • Camera & Photo Library Access: Requested only when you scan a specimen or import a mushroom photo. Images are accessed solely to facilitate on-demand identification.
  • Ephemeral AI Vision Processing: When you initiate an identification, your photo is encrypted via HTTPS and transmitted to Google Gemini AI. Processing is strictly ephemeral: images are analyzed in memory and are never permanently retained, stored on external databases, or used for public machine learning training.
  • Local Foraging Journal: Saved mushroom finds, photos, notes, and geographic coordinates are stored locally on your device storage (UserDefaults/Sandbox) and remain under your complete control.
  • Subscription & Purchase Details: All financial transactions and in-app subscriptions are handled directly by Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat. We never collect, process, or store credit card or banking information.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Data processed by Mushroom ID is used solely to:

  • Classify wild mushrooms, toadstools, brackets, and fungi using advanced visual morphological models.
  • Deliver crucial safety advisories, deadly lookalike comparisons, spore print colors, and hymenium classifications.
  • Validate Pro subscription entitlements (Mycologist Pro) and enforce rate limits.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, license, rent, or trade your personal data. We partner only with reputable, privacy-compliant service providers:

  • Google Gemini API: For secure, ephemeral multimodal AI vision analysis.
  • RevenueCat: For verifying in-app purchase receipts and entitlement state.
  • Apple Inc.: For app delivery, in-app purchases, and iOS system services.

4. User Rights & Permission Controls

You maintain full authority over permissions and stored information:

  • Revoke Camera/Photo Access: Accessible at any time via iOS Settings > Privacy > Camera / Photos.
  • Revoke AI Cloud Processing: You can toggle or revoke AI processing consent at any time inside the App under Profile > AI Privacy.
  • Delete Foraging Records: Remove any saved specimens from your journal with a single tap.

5. Children's Privacy

Mushroom ID is intended for general audiences and outdoor nature enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with an updated revision date.
