Privacy Policy

Pet Identifier: Dog & Cat Breed AI · Last Updated: September 2026
Overview: Pet Identifier ("we", "our", or "the App"), operated by ORX Labs, values your privacy and is committed to protecting the data of our users. This policy details our data practices, permissions usage, and our strict commitment to zero personal data retention.
Veterinary & Healthcare Disclaimer: Pet Identifier is designed solely for informational, educational, and entertainment purposes. AI breed analysis, mixed-breed lineage estimates, temperament indicators, and health suggestions do NOT constitute veterinary advice, diagnosis, or clinical genetic testing. Always consult a licensed veterinary medical professional for health, dietary, or behavioral decisions.

1. Information We Collect & Process

We believe in data minimization. We only access and process information strictly required to identify dog and cat breeds and manage your saved pet pack:

  • Camera & Photo Library: With your explicit consent, the App accesses your device camera or photo library solely to capture or select photographs of dogs and cats for identification.
  • AI Vision Processing: When you scan a pet, the selected photo is transmitted securely via TLS 1.3 encrypted connections to our AI inference partner (Google Gemini API). Processing is strictly ephemeral—images are analyzed in real time to generate breed classification and are immediately discarded. Photos are never stored on public servers, sold, or used to train public machine learning models.
  • No Human Biometric Tracking: The App does not perform facial recognition on humans, nor does it collect biometric identifiers of people.
  • Local Pet Pack & Sightings: Scanned pets, custom notes, and local thumbnails are stored strictly on your device using on-device local storage. They never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to share an image report.
  • Purchase & Subscription Information: Subscription entitlements and in-app purchases are securely validated via Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat. We never collect or have access to your credit card or financial information.

2. Purpose of Processing

Your inputs are processed exclusively to:

  • Identify purebred and mixed-breed dogs and cats with computer vision models.
  • Deconstruct crossbreed heritage and calculate estimated parent breed percentages.
  • Provide breed-specific care, grooming routines, temperament summaries, and exercise requirements.
  • Verify active Pro subscription access and unlock unlimited scans.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work exclusively with trusted, industry-leading infrastructure partners:

  • Google Gemini API: Ephemeral visual identification and veterinary taxonomy synthesis.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: In-app purchase receipt validation and subscription lifecycle management.
  • Apple Inc.: App distribution, cloud infrastructure, and StoreKit transaction processing.

4. Data Retention & Security

We do not operate backend user tracking databases. All your scanned pet records remain on your local device. If you delete a pet from your pack or uninstall the App, all associated local data is erased immediately.

5. User Rights & Permission Revocation

You maintain complete control over your data at all times:

  • Camera / Photos: Revoke access anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera / Photos.
  • AI Processing Consent: Revoke or re-grant AI processing consent anytime inside the App under Profile > AI Privacy.
  • Pack Deletion: Clear all history with a single tap in the App's Profile settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general family audiences and pet lovers of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
