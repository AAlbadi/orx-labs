Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 7, 2026
Website: https://orxlabs.com

ORX Labs ("we", "our", or "us") operates Content101 and related UGC content generation and automation tools. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you connect and use our application in connection with third-party platforms, including TikTok.

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate and interact with our application through TikTok services, we may collect the following:

  • TikTok Account Information: Through the TikTok Login Kit, we receive basic user profile information (such as your TikTok username, display name, user ID, and avatar) authorized by you under the user.info.basic scope.
  • Authentication Tokens: OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens necessary to maintain authorized API sessions on your behalf.
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Video files, images, slideshows, post titles, descriptions, and hashtags you explicitly choose to generate, schedule, or publish through our platform.
  • Usage Logs: Diagnostic information such as API request timestamps, publishing statuses, and error reports to ensure service uptime.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to provide and improve our services, specifically to:

  • Authenticate your account identity via TikTok Login Kit.
  • Upload and publish user-approved short videos and photo slideshows to your TikTok profile using the TikTok Content Posting API (video.upload, video.publish).
  • Allow you to review, inspect, and configure privacy levels (e.g., Public, Draft/Self-Only) before any media is posted.
  • Provide status updates on your published content.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We interact with third-party services exclusively to fulfill your requests:

  • TikTok API: All interactions with TikTok are conducted in compliance with the TikTok Developer Terms of Service and API policies.

4. Data Retention & Security

Access tokens are stored in secure, encrypted environments and are only retained for as long as necessary to provide scheduled publishing services. Temporary video and image rendering files are purged after processing.

5. User Control & Data Deletion

You retain full control over your connected TikTok account and data:

  • Revoking Access: You can disconnect ORX Labs / Content101 at any time directly through your TikTok account settings under Settings & Privacy > Security > Manage App Permissions.
  • Requesting Deletion: You may request complete deletion of any stored data associated with your account by emailing us at support@orxlabs.com. Requests are processed within 30 days.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
