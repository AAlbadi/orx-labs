Privacy Policy

Rock & Crystal AI Identifier · Last Updated: September 2026
Overview: Rock & Crystal AI Identifier ("the App"), provided and operated by ORX Labs ("we", "our", or "us"), is dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring complete transparency. This policy outlines how data is handled when you use our iOS application.

1. Information We Collect & How We Use It

We believe in strict minimal data collection. We only process information essential to delivering fast, accurate geological and mineralogical identification:

  • Camera & Photo Library: With your explicit consent, the App accesses your camera or selected photo library images solely to capture or import photos of rocks, minerals, gemstones, geodes, and crystals for identification.
  • AI Vision Processing: When you initiate a specimen identification, the photo is transmitted via encrypted HTTPS directly to our secure AI vision processing endpoint (Google Gemini API). Processing is ephemeral—specimen images are analyzed in memory in real time and are never permanently stored on remote servers or used to train public AI models.
  • Local Specimen Vault: Specimens you choose to save, including thumbnails and geological reports, are stored strictly on your local iOS device storage (`UserDefaults` and device cache). They are never synced to public servers.
  • In-App Purchases & Subscriptions: Subscription transactions and receipts are validated securely through Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat. We never collect or have access to your credit card, debit card, or banking details.

2. Purpose of Processing

All data processed by the App is used solely to:

  • Classify geological specimens and determine mineral family, chemical formula, Mohs hardness, and crystal lattice structure.
  • Generate educational reports detailing geological formation history, luster, streak powder, and origin mining locations.
  • Verify active subscription entitlements and unlock Pro features.

3. Third-Party Services

We partner only with vetted, industry-leading infrastructure providers that maintain strict data protection standards:

  • Google Cloud (Gemini Vision API): For automated real-time visual mineral analysis.
  • RevenueCat: For managing in-app subscription entitlements and receipt verification.
  • Apple Inc.: For app delivery, in-app billing transactions, and StoreKit management.

4. Your Rights & Privacy Controls

You have complete control over your data and device permissions at all times:

  • Camera & Photo Permissions: You can revoke Camera or Photo Library access at any time through your iOS Settings > Privacy > Camera / Photos.
  • AI Processing Consent: You can toggle or revoke AI specimen processing permission directly in the App under Profile > AI & Privacy.
  • Local Data Deletion: You can delete any saved specimen or clear your entire collection from your local vault with a single tap.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for students, rockhounds, and general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted directly to this URL with an updated revision date.
